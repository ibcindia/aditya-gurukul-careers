About Aditya Gurukul Careers

Our purpose

A dedicated platform for education careers.

Aditya Gurukul Careers is designed to connect education-sector professionals with relevant career opportunities. It aims to make candidate discovery, vacancy information and recruitment communication clearer and more accessible.

Our focus

  • Clear role descriptions and eligibility information
  • Structured candidate profiles and application tracking
  • Opportunities across academic and campus functions
  • Responsible handling of candidate information
