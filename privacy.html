Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how information is collected, used and managed on Aditya Gurukul Careers.

Aditya Gurukul Careers is an education career and recruitment portal intended to connect education professionals with relevant career opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit or use our website, create an account, maintain a candidate profile or apply for a job.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, mobile number and other contact details.
  • Account information: Login and authentication-related information, including information made available through Google Sign-In.
  • Educational details: Qualifications, degrees, institutions, subjects and other academic information.
  • Professional details: Work experience, current designation, skills and employment history.
  • Application information: Job preferences, applications and recruitment-related communication.
  • Documents: Resume, certificates and other documents you choose to submit.
  • Technical information: Certain device, browser and usage information that may be collected through website technologies or service providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing candidate accounts.
  • Maintaining candidate profiles and professional records.
  • Processing job applications submitted through the portal.
  • Communicating about job opportunities, applications and recruitment updates.
  • Allowing authorised recruitment personnel to review relevant candidate information.
  • Improving website functionality, services and user experience.
  • Preventing unauthorised access, misuse and fraudulent activity.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Google Sign-In and Authentication

Our portal may provide Google Sign-In as an authentication option. When you choose this option, Google may share certain account information, such as your name, email address and profile image, subject to the permissions you grant and Google's applicable policies.

Authentication services are provided through Firebase Authentication. We do not receive your Google account password through Google Sign-In.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

The portal uses or may use third-party technology services, including Google Firebase, for authentication and database-related functionality. Information processed through these services is subject to the applicable service terms and privacy policies.

We aim to restrict access to personal information to authorised personnel who need it for legitimate portal or recruitment purposes.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell candidate personal information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Authorised administrators and recruitment personnel.
  • Relevant employers or recruitment partners where necessary for a job application or with appropriate permission.
  • Technology and service providers supporting portal operations.
  • Government authorities or other parties where required by applicable law.

We aim to limit such sharing to information relevant to the purpose for which it is shared.

6. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their login credentials confidential and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.

7. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide portal services, manage recruitment activities, maintain relevant records or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required should be deleted or anonymised in accordance with applicable retention practices and legal requirements.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to personal information associated with your account.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion of information or account closure, where legally permissible.
  • Clarification about how your information is being used.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate record-keeping purposes.

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website or its service providers may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to support authentication, security and website functionality.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website features.

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites. Please review their respective privacy policies before sharing information.

11. Children's Privacy

The portal is intended for individuals who are legally eligible to use the relevant recruitment services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology or applicable legal requirements. The revised policy will be published on this page.

13. Contact and Privacy Requests

For questions, correction requests or privacy-related concerns, please contact us through our official Contact page.
